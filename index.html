Travel: Stats tracker

Every country you’ve been to, every trip and every flight, on a map that replays them.

Coming to the App Store Free · iPhone · no account, no ads

Also in the app

  • Record your trips — the countries you stayed in, the flights you took and what it cost. Your flights list is built from your trips, so there is nothing to enter twice.
  • Every airport and airline, counted — how often you’ve been through each airport and flown with each airline.
  • A globe that replays your flights — spin it, then watch every route drawn again in the order you flew it.
  • iCloud sync, no account — your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. Nothing to sign up for, and nobody else can read it.
  • Hikes and banknotes, if you want them — log your hikes, and collect a banknote from every currency. Both are optional.
