Common questions

Where is my data kept? Does it sync?

On your iPhone and in the private database of your own iCloud account, which syncs it to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Settings → iCloud shows the sync status. Nobody else, the developer included, can read it.

How do I back up my data, or move it?

Settings → Data → Export backup writes a readable JSON file with everything, banknote photos included; Send a copy shares the same file by AirDrop, Mail or Messages. Import backup reads it back, either merged with what is there or replacing everything, and shows you what will change before it does.

How are countries and days counted?

A country counts as visited when a trip has a stay there. Home countries, added in Settings, count as visited from the date you moved there, but never add days traveled. Days are counted inclusively, so a trip from Friday to Sunday is three days.

Where are Hikes and Banknotes?

Both are off by default. Turn one on in Settings → Features. They share the tab bar's last place, so turning one on turns the other off.

An airline or airport is missing

Add it in Settings under Custom airlines or Custom airports. It then appears in the pickers and on the map, and syncs like your trips.

The exchange rate isn't showing

Rates are fetched from public services when you open a country, at most about once a day, so the first time needs a connection. Otherwise the app shows the last rates it received.

I deleted something by mistake

Swiping a record away offers Undo for a few seconds. Past that, the way back is a backup, which is the argument for exporting one occasionally.

Reporting something wrong

If a number looks incorrect, that is worth an email. Include the screen, what it shows and what you expected. An exported backup makes the cause much easier to find, but it holds your whole travel history, so only send one if you are comfortable with that, and never post one publicly.
